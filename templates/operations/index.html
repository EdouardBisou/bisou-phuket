Behind the scenes

How Bisou Phuket runs its back office on Mise

Bisou Phuket runs its restaurant back office on Mise, restaurant back-office software for owner-run restaurants and groups in Southeast Asia, made by SETS Foundry Limited.

Bisou Phuket uses Mise to keep the parts of the restaurant that guests never see in order: who is on shift, which supplier invoices are due, and how each night went. It is the same quiet system behind the service on Cherngtalay, Phuket.

What the team uses it for

  • Rosters and hours. Shifts are published to the team and staff clock in from their phones.
  • Supplier invoices. Invoices sit in one book with their photos or PDFs, payment status and due dates.
  • The nightly numbers. Each night's sales are read from the end-of-day reports instead of being retyped.

What Mise is

Mise is back-office software for owner-run restaurants and groups in Southeast Asia. Beyond the three areas above, it builds the monthly profit and loss, splits payments between partners, costs recipes and counts cellar stock. It is made by SETS Foundry Limited, a company based in Hong Kong.

To see what it does for a restaurant, visit mise.systems.
