## When is Bisou Phuket open on New Year's Eve 2026?

**Bisou Phuket is open on New Year's Eve, Thursday 31 December 2026, from 5:00 PM, with the kitchen open until midnight.** On a normal night we open at 6:00 PM and take the last food order around 11 PM, so for New Year's Eve the doors open an hour earlier and the kitchen stays open an hour later.

That gives two kinds of evening. Come at 5 PM with the children, when an early table suits them best. Or take the beach and the sunset first, change, and still sit down to a full French dinner at ten and run it through to the new year.

## How late can I eat on New Year's Eve?

**At Bisou Phuket the kitchen takes orders until midnight on 31 December 2026, and the cocktail bar runs to 1:00 AM.** On an ordinary night, most kitchens around Laguna and Bang Tao stop serving food by 11 PM, which is what we found when we checked them for our guide to [dinner outside your Laguna resort](/journal/dinner-off-resort-laguna-phuket). So on New Year's Eve a 10 PM table is no rush here: the kitchen keeps taking orders through to midnight, and the first glass of the year is a few steps away at the bar.

## Who is Bisou Phuket?

**Bisou Phuket is a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar in Cherngtalay, opened in 2025 by chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne, with head chef Manon Bares running the kitchen and general manager Louis Chartier looking after the room.** Bisou means kiss in French and the tagline is simple & sexy.
