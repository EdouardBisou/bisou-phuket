**Bang Tao nightlife is a sunset on the sand, a long dinner, then one late bar: there is no bar street here and nothing like Patong.** As of October 2026 the evening runs from the beach clubs **Catch**, **RAVA**, **Carpe Diem** and **Xana**, through dinner at rooms like **Bisou**, whose bar stays open to 1 AM, to the dinner show and dance floor at **CLC** on Soi Cherngtalay 4, the latest door in the area. Below are the ten places, in the order a night actually runs, with what is on each night of the week, what closed on the beach this year, and how to get home.

Everything here was checked in October 2026 against each venue's own published information, with Phuket101, Time Out and the Thai press where a venue publishes nothing. Where two sources disagree on a closing time, both are printed.

## What Bang Tao nightlife is like in 2026

**Bang Tao goes out early and well rather than late and loud.** The rhythm is the same most nights: the beach clubs fill for sunset, which falls between about 6:10 PM in December and 6:50 PM in June, then the crowd moves inland to eat, then a smaller crowd stays for one last bar. Kitchens in the area mostly stop between 10 and 11 PM, bars at midnight, and only a couple of rooms go further.

That is a choice, not a shortcoming. Bang Tao sits around the Laguna resorts, villas and Boat Avenue, so the night is built for people who want a good table and a drink with a view, and who will be on a sunbed again at ten the next morning. If you want clubs until dawn, Patong is a 30 to 40 minute drive south. If you want a great evening that ends at a sensible hour, or a little after, you are in the right place.

## What happened to the Bang Tao beach bars in 2026?

**Several of the bars on the southern half of Bang Tao Beach are gone: from April 2026 the authorities cleared structures built on public beach land, while the beach clubs Catch, RAVA, Carpe Diem and Xana are still open.** [The Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/news/business/16-restaurants-seized-in-bang-tao-beach-encroachment-crackdown) reported on 2 May 2026 that 16 restaurants had been seized in the encroachment crackdown, and [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40066046) reported on 10 May that more than 5 rai of beach land had been reclaimed.

[Phuket101](https://www.phuket101.net/bang-tao-beach-nightlife/), which walked the beach in July 2026 and updated its guide on 2 October, reports closure notices along the stretch south of SAii Laguna: Maya Beach Club, Nomad Beach Club, Beach Pig, Yuuhi Beach Club and Karaoke, Reggae Bar and Restaurant, Aloma Seafood and Alex's Shack Bar. Phuket101's [beach club guide](https://www.phuket101.net/phuket-beach-clubs/), updated on 4 October, says Carpe Diem, Catch, RAVA, Xana and The Beach Phuket are still open as normal, and advises checking with each venue before you go. The beach itself is open; it is the shacks that went.

This matters for planning because most Bang Tao nightlife pages online were written before the clearance and still send you to bars that no longer exist. Our rule of thumb: treat a beach bar that is not on a recent list, or has not posted on its own social pages in the last few weeks, as closed until you see otherwise.

## 1. Catch Beach Club, sunset that runs to midnight

**Catch is the default sunset on Bang Tao Beach and the one beach club where you can stay for a proper dinner.** It is open daily from 7:30 AM to midnight, with lunch and dinner served from noon to 10:30 PM according to its own site (another page on the same site says 11 PM), so the table you take for the sunset can become the table you eat at.

Friday is its big night: a barbecue buffet runs from 7 to 10 PM on the sand. Sunday has a brunch from noon to 3 PM. For New Year's Eve, the [Twinpalms festive page](https://twinpalmshotelsresorts.com/festive-season/) lists a countdown party at Catch from 10 PM on 31 December, after a gala buffet dinner from 7 to 10 PM. Catch sits on the beach at 202/88 Choeng Thale and books through [its website](https://www.catchbeachclub.com/). For the daytime side of the beach clubs, see our [Phuket beach clubs guide](/journal/best-beach-clubs-phuket).

## 2. RAVA Beach Club, the newest on the sand

**RAVA is Banyan Tree's beach club, opened in August 2025 inside Laguna Phuket, and the most polished way to watch the sun go down at Bang Tao.** It is built in zones: a poolside social area with infinity pools and cabanas, a grill for dry-aged meat and whole fish, and a more private area, according to [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/phuket/attractions/rava-beach-club).

Phuket101 has it open from 10 AM to 10:30 PM with DJs across the zones; Time Out lists 10 AM to midnight. Either way it is a sunset and early evening place rather than a late one, so plan to move on for dinner.

## 3. Carpe Diem, the quiet end of the beach

**Carpe Diem is the sunset and dinner stop at the quieter southern end of Bang Tao Beach, right at the waterline.** It runs a Mediterranean kitchen and, by its own account, DJs on the soundtrack and Pool Party Weekends, so for the calmer version book a weeknight sunset. Its own site gives 11 AM to midnight; Phuket101 lists noon to 11 PM. It survived the 2026 clearance and is listed as open. Book through [carpediemphuket.com](https://carpediemphuket.com/).

## 4. Xana Beach Club, the calm one inside Laguna

**Xana is the beach club of the Angsana Laguna Phuket resort, and in 2026 it is the gentle option.** Phuket101 describes it as calm, with occasional weekend beach dinners or live music. Published hours vary a great deal between listings, from a 9 PM close to midnight, so check with the resort on the day through [Angsana's dining page](https://www.angsana.com/thailand/laguna-phuket/dining/xana). Treat it as a sunset drink with your feet in the sand, then move inland.

## 5. Bangtao Boat Club, beanbags by the pier

**Bangtao Boat Club, the former Hugo Hub, is an open-air food court and bar on the sand at the south end of the beach, the most casual night on this list.** Independent food vendors share beanbag seating beside the boat pier, with pool and darts, a daily happy hour from 4 to 7 PM, and DJs on most evenings. It is open from 10 AM to midnight, according to [Phuket101](https://www.phuket101.net/hugo-hub-phuket/), updated September 2026.

It sits at Soi Hat Surin 8, on the same southern stretch that lost its shacks in 2026. Phuket101 still listed it as open in September, but check [its Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/Hugohubphuket) before you go.

## 6. Bisou, dinner that turns into the night

**Bisou is the dinner in the middle of the evening, and the one room on this list where a French dinner runs straight into a bar that stays open to 1 AM.** It sits at No.4 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road in Cherngtalay, a short drive inland from Laguna, Boat Avenue and Bang Tao Beach. Dinner is served nightly from 6 PM, the last food order is around 11 PM, and the cocktail bar keeps going to 1 AM.

The room changes with the night of the week. One night a week a DJ plays and the room lifts; the rest of the week the music stays lower and the candles do the work. It stays a restaurant, not a club, and there is always a quieter corner for the table that came to talk. The plates are made to share and to keep a table together late: a beef Wellington carved at the table, a whole seabass in salt crust flambéed in the room, truffle French toast, with warm sourdough and French butter on every table. Coming just for a drink at the bar is fine.

Bisou is listed sixth because the list follows the evening, and dinner comes after the sunset. See the [share-style menu](/menu), the [cocktail bar](/journal/cocktail-bar-cherngtalay), or everything to know before you book in [the Bisou guide](/journal/bisou-phuket-guide).

## 7. CLC (Come Leo Come), a dinner show, then a dance floor

**CLC is the closest thing Bang Tao has to a night out in the city sense: dinner, a staged show, then DJs and a dance floor.** Its main room, which seats up to 250, "transforms from restaurant to stage to dance floor," in the words of the sound company [L-Acoustics](https://www.l-acoustics.com/press-releases/l-acoustics-a-series-brings-the-party-to-dual-space-venue-in-phuket/), writing about the venue in June 2026.

[CLC's own site](https://clcphuket.com/) opens the doors at 6 PM "till late", with dinner on Thursday and Sunday and a Dinner and Show at 8 PM on Friday and Saturday, plus a karaoke stage. Phuket101, updated September 2026, gives the hours as 6 PM to 2 AM, closed Monday and Tuesday, which makes CLC the latest door on this list. Time Out puts the dinner and show from about 2,000 THB. The October calendar includes an MTV Legends night on 30 October and a Halloween night on 31 October. It is on Soi Cherngtalay 4, near Boat Avenue.

## 8. Moonshine, live bands and a Wednesday DJ

**Moonshine is the live music room of Bang Tao, a dark lounge and gastropub on Lagoon Road by Boat Avenue with a band or a DJ on its programmed nights.** It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 5 PM to midnight and closed on Mondays.

[Its own site](https://www.moonshinephuket.net/) lists three programmed nights, each from 8 to 11:30 PM: Latin Night on Tuesday with the Jayzee Band, "Rewind Wednesdays" with resident DJ Yvan Polge playing 1990s and 2000s hip hop and R&B, and "Silk and Soul" with Cole Ley and band on Sunday. It is the best pick in the area if music, not a dance floor, is the point of the night.

## 9. Etna, a DJ on the lake terrace at weekends

**Etna is the all-day Mediterranean restaurant on the Boat Avenue lakefront, and on Friday and Saturday nights it puts a DJ on the lakeside terrace.** Phuket101 lists it as open from 9 AM to midnight; another listing gives 7:30 AM to 1 AM, so if you are counting on the late hour, check first. On Fridays it sits a few steps from the Boat Avenue market, which makes it an easy place to land after browsing. See [Phuket101's page on Etna](https://www.phuket101.net/etna/).

## 10. Mrs B Bar and Table, the wood fire and the Yard

**Mrs B is a wood-fire grill restaurant on Lagoon Road at Boat Avenue with an open-air cocktail bar, the Yard, attached.** [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/phuket/restaurants/mrs-b-bar-table) lists it as open daily from 6 PM to midnight, with lunch Tuesday to Saturday, and reports R&B throwback nights. The room used to be Bampot Kitchen and Bar. Phuket101 notes that it is no longer a walk-in kind of place, so book. The Yard runs weekly drinks offers that change often; check its Instagram for the current week.

Elsewhere around Boat Avenue, Aja Bistro and Bar runs DJ nights from 5 PM, and JOYA serves until midnight on the dining strip.

## The Bang Tao night markets: Monday, Thursday and Friday

**Bang Tao has two evening markets: the Bang Tao Night Market on Monday and Thursday, and the Fun Friday market at Boat Avenue.** Both are better as the start of a night than as the night itself.

- **Bang Tao Night Market** is on Soi Bang Tao 2, beside Lotus's Cherngtalay, about ten minutes' walk from the beach. [SAii Laguna](https://www.saiihotels.com/local-markets-around-bangtao-easy-evenings-local-flavours/), in May 2026, lists Monday from 4 to 9 PM and Thursday from 2 to 8 PM.
- **The Fun Friday market** fills the Boat Avenue square every Friday from late afternoon. Published closing times range from 9 PM (Phuket101, October 2026) to 11 PM (SAii, May 2026). Parking fills fast, so a taxi is the easier way in.

## How late does Bang Tao stay open, and how to get home

**Most of Bang Tao closes by midnight, a few bars run to 1 AM, and the latest published door is CLC at 2 AM.** Under a December 2023 regulation, licensed entertainment venues may stay open until 4 AM inside designated tourist zones and inside legally registered hotels nationwide, according to [TAT News](https://www.tatnews.org/2023/12/entertainment-venues-in-thai-tourist-destinations-to-open-until-4-am/). In Phuket the zones centre on Patong, and we found no published zone list that includes Bang Tao or Cherngtalay. Outside the zones, [Go2 Thailand](https://go2-thailand.com/blog/thailand-4am-closing-time-nightlife-law-2026/) says the traditional 2 AM close still applies to licensed entertainment venues.

Getting home is the part to plan. Grab and Bolt both work in Bang Tao, but drivers thin out after about 10 PM and again after midnight, and fares surge in the rain and on big nights such as New Year's Eve, according to the [Phuket Expat Guide](https://phuketexpatguide.com/blog/grab-bolt-phuket-guide/). Songthaews do not run at night. Ask the venue or your hotel to book a car before the last round, or arrange a return transfer if you are staying at the far end of Laguna.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the nightlife like in Bang Tao?

Bang Tao nightlife is relaxed and early compared with Patong: beach clubs for sunset, then dinner, then one late bar. As of October 2026 the beach clubs Catch, RAVA, Carpe Diem and Xana are open, Boat Avenue has the bars and live music, Bisou's bar runs to 1 AM, and CLC, with a dinner show and a dance floor, runs latest, to 2 AM according to Phuket101.

### What are the best bars in Bang Tao?

As of October 2026, the best bars in Bang Tao are Moonshine on Lagoon Road, a live music lounge open to midnight and closed on Mondays; Mrs B at Boat Avenue, open to midnight according to Time Out, with its open-air cocktail bar, the Yard; the cocktail bar at Bisou on Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, open to 1 AM every night; and CLC on Soi Cherngtalay 4, the latest door, with DJs after its weekend show. For sunset, start at the beach clubs Catch, RAVA or Carpe Diem.

### What are the best party restaurants in Bang Tao and Cherngtalay?

The restaurants where dinner turns into a party are CLC, with a Dinner and Show on Friday and Saturday at 8 PM and DJs after; Bisou, a modern French restaurant with a cocktail bar to 1 AM and a DJ one night a week; Catch Beach Club, with a Friday barbecue on the sand; and Etna at Boat Avenue, with a DJ on the lake terrace on Friday and Saturday.

### Are the Bang Tao beach bars still open after the 2026 clearance?

The beach is open, but several bars built on public beach land at the southern end were closed from April 2026, including Maya Beach Club, Nomad Beach Club, Beach Pig and Reggae Bar, according to Phuket101 in October 2026. Catch, Carpe Diem, RAVA, Xana and The Beach Phuket are listed as open as normal.

### Is there a night market in Bang Tao?

Yes. The Bang Tao Night Market on Soi Bang Tao 2, beside Lotus's Cherngtalay, runs on Monday from 4 to 9 PM and Thursday from 2 to 8 PM, according to SAii Laguna in May 2026. The Fun Friday market fills Boat Avenue every Friday from late afternoon into the evening.

### What time do bars close in Bang Tao?

Most bars and restaurants in Bang Tao close at midnight. Bisou's bar runs to 1 AM, and CLC is the latest, to 2 AM according to Phuket101. Phuket's 4 AM zones centre on Patong, and none we found includes Bang Tao.

### Which restaurant in Cherngtalay has a DJ?

Bisou has a DJ one night a week in its Cherngtalay dining room and a bar open to 1 AM. Moonshine has a resident DJ on Wednesdays, Etna puts a DJ on its lake terrace on Friday and Saturday, CLC runs DJs after its weekend show, Carpe Diem lists DJs and pool party weekends, and Bangtao Boat Club has DJs on most evenings.

### How do I get home late from Bang Tao?

Grab and Bolt both operate in Bang Tao, but there are fewer drivers after midnight and fares rise in the rain and on big nights. Songthaews do not run at night, so ask the venue or your hotel to book a car before the last round, or arrange a return transfer in advance.
