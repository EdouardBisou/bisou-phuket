The most romantic restaurants near Bang Tao and Cherngtalay are within fifteen minutes of the Laguna gates: **PRU** at Trisara, the only restaurant on Phuket holding a MICHELIN star, **Saffron** overlooking the lagoons at Banyan Tree, **Bisou** in Cherngtalay for candlelight and a cocktail bar that runs to 1 AM, **Suay** for twenty-six tables in a lit garden, and **Catch** and **Carpe Diem** for dinner on the sand at Bang Tao. Ten rooms follow, with the price of dinner, the table to ask for, and the thing that trips couples up when they book.

This is the west coast, so the sun sets into the sea in front of you between about 6:10 PM in December and 6:50 PM in June, and almost every good decision here starts with that. Prices, hours and closures were checked against each restaurant's own published information in September 2026.

## What makes a Bang Tao dinner romantic

**Romance around Bang Tao comes in three settings: a table on the sand at Bang Tao Beach for the sunset, a garden or a lagoon away from the road at Suay and Banyan Tree, and a dark candlelit room like Bisou or Moonshine once the beach has gone cold.** The list below runs through all three, plus two that are worth the short drive inland.

One piece of planning matters more here than anywhere else on the island. Sunset is the event, so if you want it, book the first seating and arrive thirty minutes before the sun goes down on the day; if you want quiet instead, book at 8 PM, when the beach clubs have turned their music up and the garden rooms have turned theirs down. High season, from November to April, fills the good tables days ahead. And note the format before you choose: some of these are full dinner restaurants, others are drinks and small plates with a view. For the wider area beyond romance, see our guide to [where to eat in Bang Tao](/journal/where-to-eat-bang-tao).

## 1. PRU, the island's only MICHELIN-starred dinner

**PRU at Trisara is the special-occasion table on Phuket: the island's only restaurant holding a MICHELIN star in the [MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2026](https://guide.michelin.com/th/en/phuket-region/phuket/restaurant/pru), which also awards it a Green Star for sustainability, on a private bay just north of Bang Tao.** The PRU Experience tasting menu is 7,500 THB++ per person from 1 October 2026.

Read that date carefully, because it is the thing to know this month: **PRU is closed for its annual break until 1 October 2026.** When PRU reopens, dinner runs Tuesday to Saturday with fixed seatings at 6:00, 6:30 and 7:00 PM, in a small solar-panelled room by the sea where outdoor tables catch the sunset. Everything on the plate is sourced in Thailand, much of it from the restaurant's own farm, PRU Jampa, and Trisara describes the evening as a candid exchange between chefs and guests: "our kitchen is your dining table."

PRU books out most nights in high season, so reserve thirty to sixty days ahead and ask for an outdoor table on the sea side. The tenth-anniversary week, The Gathering, runs 27 October to 1 November 2026 with guest chefs and books separately. Trisara is ten to fifteen minutes north of Laguna by car. [prurestaurant.com](https://www.prurestaurant.com/)

## 2. Saffron, a table over the lagoons

**Saffron is the quietest resort dinner in Laguna: Banyan Tree's signature Thai restaurant, seating just 92, with tables looking over the resort's saltwater lagoons.** The set menus run about 2,300 THB net per person, with à la carte mains roughly 550 to 1,070 THB net; the seasonal Table of Legends guest-chef dinners are ticketed separately at 4,900 THB net.

Saffron's dining room was redesigned in March 2026, and Banyan Tree was careful to keep what made it work, the serene lagoon views, while adding two private dining rooms for celebrations. This is the original Saffron, the restaurant that grew from a curry house into the flagship Thai dining room of the whole Banyan Tree group, so the cooking is traditional recipes given contemporary plating rather than an experiment.

Saffron serves dinner only, daily 6:30 PM to 10:30 PM, smart casual. Ask for a lagoon-side table, or one of the two private rooms if you are marking something; for a larger group, compare the options in our guide to [private dining in Bang Tao and Cherngtalay](/journal/private-dining-bang-tao-cherngtalay). The same resort runs a Mekhala sunset cruise on the lagoon and a private in-villa seafood barbecue for proposals. [banyantree.com](https://www.banyantree.com/thailand/phuket/dining/saffron)

## 3. Bisou, candlelight and a bar until 1 AM

**Bisou is the candlelit dinner in Cherngtalay: a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar at No.4 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, plated to share, a few minutes from Laguna, Boat Avenue and Bang Tao Beach.** Signature dishes run 1,290 to 2,990 THB and mains 490 to 990 THB, and the bar stays open until 1 AM.

Bisou was built for two: a wall of kisses, a spiral staircase, low light, and a menu written for two pairs of hands. Share the truffle French toast (390 THB) to start, then the beef Wellington (2,990 THB) carved at the table or the whole seabass in salt crust (1,290 THB), flambéed in front of you. Chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne founded Bisou in 2025, head chef Manon Bares runs the kitchen and Louis Chartier the room.

Two things make Bisou work for a couple staying nearby. The kitchen takes its last food order around 11 PM but the bar carries on to 1 AM, so dinner does not have to end when the plates go, which is rare here and the subject of its own guide to [eating late in Bang Tao](/journal/late-night-dining-bang-tao). And during your [birthday month](/journal/birthday-dinner-phuket) the whole table gets 10 percent off the bill on presentation of an ID, with the birthday dessert plate on the house and no charge if you bring your own cake. Ask for a table against the kiss wall, or a stool at the bar if you would rather watch the room. Terrace in front, parking close by the restaurant, French, English and Thai spoken. [Everything to know before you book](/journal/bisou-phuket-guide), or see the [menu](/menu).

## 4. Suay Cherngtalay, a garden of twenty-six tables

**Suay Cherngtalay sets twenty-six tables in a tropical garden inside Baan Wana Park, with the lights coming on one by one at dusk, and is listed in the MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2026 as Suay (Choeng Thale).** Sharing set menus for two start at 2,199 THB, so about 1,100 THB a head.

Chef Tammasak "Noi" Chootong cooks Thai contemporary at Suay, Thai ingredients through European technique, and the set menus are built exactly for two people who want to try everything: six dishes, with signatures like miang foie gras and sous vide beef short rib. Because Suay sits in a garden compound off Si Sunthon Road rather than on a roadside strip, the noise is cicadas rather than scooters.

Suay is open daily 4 PM to 11 PM, with a live band on Monday and Thursday nights, so pick another night if you want to talk. Book through TableCheck at least 24 hours ahead and ask for a garden table when you confirm; the garden zone permits smoking, which is worth knowing. Suay is ten minutes inland from Bang Tao Beach. [suayrestaurant.com](https://www.suayrestaurant.com/)

## 5. Catch Beach Club, dinner on Bang Tao sand

**Catch Beach Club puts tables on wooden decks under casuarina trees directly on Bang Tao sand, facing due west, so dinner opens with the sunset.** The Friday barbecue dinner is 2,190 THB including wine; à la carte runs higher.

Catch opened at Surin in 2008 as Phuket's first beach club and has been on Bang Tao under the Twinpalms flag since the end of 2016, and it knows exactly what it is: glamorous and lively rather than hushed, with DJs, themed nights and a central bar built for the sunset drink before you sit down. The Catch kitchen serves until 10:30 PM and the club stays open to midnight.

Book Catch ahead through SevenRooms, essential at weekends and through high season, and ask for a deck table on the sand rather than the pool row. Evenings are smart casual. If your idea of romantic is candles and quiet, take Carpe Diem below or one of the garden rooms instead; if it is a bottle, bare feet in the sand and music, this is the one. More of the same in our [Phuket beach club guide](/journal/best-beach-clubs-phuket). [catchbeachclub.com](https://www.catchbeachclub.com)

## 6. Carpe Diem, the quiet end of the beach

**Carpe Diem sits at the waterline on the quieter southern end of Bang Tao Beach, under tall coconut palms, and its fire show on the sand runs at 7 PM at the end of the Sunday La Dolce Vita day.** Mediterranean cooking, à la carte, with a wood-fired oven and seafood built for two.

The Carpe Diem dishes to share are the bouillabaisse made with local Phuket lobster, a whole Maine lobster, or a grilled branzino, with a Balearic soundtrack once the light goes. The official line is romantic dinners under the stars, and on a weeknight that is accurate.

One trade-off worth planning around: Carpe Diem's Saturday and Sunday daytimes are pool-party days with DJs, and Sunday noon to 3 PM is the lively La Dolce Vita brunch at 3,500 THB plus VAT. A weeknight sunset booking at the waterline row under the coconut palms is the calmer play, though you give up the fire show. Open daily 11 AM to midnight, reservations recommended for the sunset slot. [carpediemphuket.com](https://carpediemphuket.com/)

## 7. Moonshine, a jazz speakeasy off Boat Avenue

**Moonshine is the after-dark room: a candlelit Prohibition-style lounge on Lagoon Road at Boat Avenue, with dark walls, crimson velvet, leather banquettes and live music six nights a week from 8:00 to 11:30 PM.** Dinner at Moonshine runs about 1,000 to 2,000 THB per person.

Phuket 101 calls Moonshine the island's only true speakeasy-style bar and gastropub, and the kitchen backs the setting up with house-made pasta, steaks and seafood: share the lobster linguine at 690 THB++ with something off the Prohibition-inspired cocktail list. The jazz and swing nights are Friday, with Frankie Hong's Trio, and Saturday, with the Chill Out Jazz Band.

Timing is the whole trick at Moonshine. Book around 7 PM for a quiet candlelit dinner and ask for a banquette away from the band, or come later to eat with the music playing. Closed Mondays, otherwise 5 PM to midnight. Tuesday is Latin night and Wednesday is hip hop, so aim Thursday to Sunday for the jazz mood. Moonshine is under ten minutes from the Laguna gates; for the cocktail side of the area see our [Cherngtalay cocktail bar guide](/journal/cocktail-bar-cherngtalay). [moonshinephuket.net](https://www.moonshinephuket.net/)

## 8. Ma Doo Bua, a table over a lotus pond

**Ma Doo Bua sets Thai pavilions and terraces directly over a pond of giant Victoria amazonica water lilies, whose floating pads reach up to three metres wide.** Expect 400 to 800 THB per person, and come in daylight.

The Ma Doo Bua ritual is the reason to go: for 350 THB a staff member helps you stand on a lily pad for a photograph, on pads the venue says hold up to about 50 kilos, with drone packages of the two of you over the pond from 500 THB. Then you eat what the pond grows, deep-fried lotus-stem fries and stir-fried lotus stems with shrimp paste, at around 250 THB a plate. The whole thing sits inside a replica traditional Thai village of teak-style villas and canals.

Plan Ma Doo Bua as a late-afternoon date rather than a night out: the café serves 9 AM to 9 PM, but the lily-pad photograph and the drone package need daylight, so arrive around 4:30 to 5:30 PM and ask for a pavilion out over the pond. Weekends draw day visitors. Ma Doo Bua is ten minutes from Boat Avenue. [madoobua.com](https://www.madoobua.com/)

## 9. 360° Bar, sunset from the hill above Layan

**The 360° Bar at The Pavilions Phuket sits at the highest point of the resort on the hill above Layan, with an open deck over the Andaman and Bang Tao bay, and it does not admit children under 16.** Small plates run from about 110 to 250 THB, with cocktails around 350 THB.

Getting up to the 360° Bar is part of it: a resort buggy takes you from reception up the bamboo-lined hillside road to the deck. Happy hour runs 7:30 to 8:30 PM with half price on selected drinks, which lands exactly after the sun has gone, and the adults-only rule keeps the deck quiet.

Be clear on the format: the 360° Bar is a tapas and cocktail rooftop, not a long à la carte dinner room. Come 30 to 45 minutes before sunset for the golden hour, take a place at the western rail, then either graze there or move down to ALTO, the resort's Italian room, Tuesday to Saturday 6 to 10 PM. Open daily 5 to 10 PM, smart casual, no beachwear. The Pavilions is ten minutes north of Laguna. [pavilionshotels.com](https://www.pavilionshotels.com/destinations/phuket/the-pavilions-phuket/dining/bar-and-restaurants/)

## 10. Leilani, opera between the courses

**Leilani is a candlelit vintage gallery in Manik, inland from Cherngtalay, where a soprano sings live opera between courses at the Saturday Dinner Party.** The five-course set menu is 2,500 THB plus 10 percent per person, by reservation only, and includes a welcome drink.

The Leilani room is design, art and dining in one, and the Saturday evening is deliberately kept small, so it plays like a private dinner party rather than a performance with a meal attached. Phuket 101 described it in April 2026 as the inland room where Saturday nights turn into a dinner party with live opera and dancing. On other evenings Leilani is a quieter à la carte restaurant, Thai and European on the same card, that takes walk-ins.

Leilani is open Tuesday to Saturday, 6 to 10 PM, closed Sunday and Monday. The Saturday Dinner Party sells out, so book by the website form or WhatsApp, and ask for a table near the piano. Leilani is about ten minutes inland from Boat Avenue and the Laguna resorts, so this is the one to take a car to; for other reasons to leave the resort, see [dinner off resort near Laguna](/journal/dinner-off-resort-laguna-phuket). [leilanirestaurant.com](https://www.leilanirestaurant.com/)

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the most romantic restaurant in Bang Tao?

The most romantic restaurant in Bang Tao depends on the evening you want. PRU at Trisara is the landmark dinner, the only restaurant on Phuket holding a MICHELIN star, though it is closed until 1 October 2026. Saffron at Banyan Tree Laguna is the quiet resort table over the lagoons. Bisou in Cherngtalay is the candlelit one, minutes from Laguna and Bang Tao Beach, with a bar that runs to 1 AM.

### Where can you have dinner on the beach in Bang Tao?

Catch Beach Club and Carpe Diem both put tables on the sand at Bang Tao facing west, so dinner starts with the sunset. Catch is the livelier of the two, with DJs and a Friday barbecue at 2,190 THB. Carpe Diem sits at the quieter southern end, and its fire show runs at 7 PM on Sundays at the end of the La Dolce Vita day.

### How much does a romantic dinner for two cost near Laguna Phuket?

Dinner for two costs about 1,000 to 1,600 THB at Ma Doo Bua, 2,199 THB for the sharing set at Suay Cherngtalay, roughly 4,600 THB at Saffron on the set menu, and 15,000 THB or more at PRU on the tasting menu. At Bisou, signature dishes run 1,290 to 2,990 THB and mains 490 to 990 THB, so a shared dinner sits between those. Menus are usually quoted before a 10 percent service charge and 7 percent VAT.

### What time is sunset in Bang Tao?

Sunset over Bang Tao Beach on Phuket's west coast falls between about 6:10 PM in November and December and about 6:50 PM in June and July. Take the first seating and arrive thirty minutes before the sun goes down on the day if the view is the point. For a quieter dinner, book at 8 PM instead, once the beach clubs have turned their music up and the garden rooms have turned theirs down.

### What is the dress code for romantic restaurants near Bang Tao?

Smart casual covers every restaurant on this list. PRU at Trisara and Saffron at Banyan Tree expect closed shoes and a collar for men, the 360° Bar at The Pavilions asks for no beachwear and admits nobody under 16, and Catch Beach Club and Carpe Diem are barefoot by day and smart casual after dark. As of September 2026.

### Where can you eat late near Bang Tao and Cherngtalay?

Bisou in Cherngtalay runs latest: the kitchen takes its last food order around 11 PM and the cocktail bar stays open until 1 AM. Catch Beach Club serves until 10:30 PM and closes at midnight, Carpe Diem runs to midnight, and Moonshine at Boat Avenue closes at midnight, Tuesday to Sunday. PRU, Saffron, Suay Cherngtalay and Leilani all stop serving by 10:30 PM or earlier.

### Do romantic restaurants near Bang Tao need booking in advance?

In high season, from November to April, yes: a few days ahead for Suay Cherngtalay or Catch Beach Club, and thirty to sixty days for PRU, which seats only a handful of tables across three fixed seatings. Leilani's Saturday Dinner Party is reservation only all year. Bisou takes walk-ins when there is room, though booking is better.

### Which romantic restaurants near Bang Tao are good for a proposal?

PRU at Trisara and Saffron at Banyan Tree both do private, planned dinners, and Banyan Tree also runs a Mekhala sunset cruise on the lagoon and a private in-villa seafood barbecue marketed for proposals. Bisou can seat a couple in a quiet corner against the kiss wall, and the bar stays open afterwards.
