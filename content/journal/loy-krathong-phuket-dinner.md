## When is Loy Krathong in Phuket in 2026?

**Loy Krathong 2026 is Tuesday 24 November, the night of the full moon of the twelfth Thai lunar month.** The [TAT newsroom](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/loi-krathong-2026-lights-up-thailands-rivers-and-historic-cities/) (5 November 2026) places the full moon on 24 November 2026, [PPTV](https://www.pptvhd36.com/news/%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%8C%E0%B8%AA%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%8C/283924) gives Tuesday 24 November, and [Phuket 101](https://www.phuket101.net/loy-krathong-phuket/), updated 28 September 2026, gives the same date. Some English-language sites print 25 November; we treat 24 November as the date and suggest you confirm any ticketed event with its organiser.

The moon is at its fullest at about 9:53 PM Bangkok time (14:53 UTC, per [Star Walk](https://starwalk.space/en/news/BCD688FF-B3D7-EFE2-8680-B18F9F94C3BB)), and Phuket shares that clock. We calculate sunset on the west coast at about 6:05 PM on 24 November, so there are roughly four hours between sunset and the peak.

## What does Loy Krathong mean?

**Loy Krathong, "floating the basket", is the night Thais return a small offering of banana leaf, flowers, incense and a candle to the water.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loi_Krathong) says people thank Mae Khongkha, the water goddess, and let go of anger as the candle drifts away, and the [PPTV explainer](https://www.pptvhd36.com/news/%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%8C%E0%B8%AA%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%8C/283924) adds an apology to the goddess and respect for the Buddha's footprint. Meanings vary from household to household, and the story of a Sukhothai court lady making the first krathong is a legend, not recorded history.

## Where do people float a krathong in Phuket?

**The named gatherings are Saphan Hin in Phuket Town, Patong, Karon, Kata, Nai Harn Lake, Rawai pier and, for the west coast, the lagoons at Bang Tao and Laguna.** That list is from [Phuket 101](https://www.phuket101.net/loy-krathong-phuket/), which describes Saphan Hin as the main local gathering with a fair and krathong competitions, Patong as the largest crowd, and the Bang Tao and Laguna lagoons as the choice for anyone staying on that coast. [Kata Rocks](https://www.katarocks.com/journal/loy-krathong-phuket/) adds that Kata, Karon and Patong host gatherings at dusk.

For 2026, three listings are published:

- **Saphan Hin, Phuket Town.** The TAT lists the Phuket Traditional Tourism Festival: Loi Krathong by the Sea at Saphan Hin from 16 to 25 November 2026, described as a coastal celebration of Loi Krathong.
- **The Vijitt Resort, Rawai.** A ticketed Loy Krathong night on 24 November 2026 at THB 1,500 net per person, with a buffet dinner, Thai performances and floating krathongs. Its [Phuket.Net listing](https://www.phuket.net/events/loy-krathong-night-2026-vijitt-resort-phuket/) prints 6:30 to 9:30 PM in the header and 6:30 to 10:30 PM in the dinner section, so confirm the end time with the resort.
- **Karma Royal Boat Lagoon.** A [Karma Group package](https://karmagroup.com/karma-curated-events/phuket-nights-loy-krathong-lights/) for 23 to 25 November 2026 whose itinerary lists "Loy Krathong at Saphan Hin Park" at 6:30 PM on 24 November. The page says the event has sold out.

As of 9 November 2026 we found no published 2026 programme for Bang Tao, Surin or Layan beaches, and no municipal list of floating sites. Check with the Phuket Municipality or your hotel in the week itself. Phuket 101 puts the busiest hours at the water at roughly 8 to 10 PM and says there is no official start time.

## Will it rain on Loy Krathong night in Phuket?

**Rain showed up at Phuket International Airport on 78 of 165 evenings (47 percent) between 17 November and 1 December, 2015 to 2025, though often briefly: a date counts as rainy if any one half-hourly reading in the window carried a rain or thunderstorm code.** The count covers 2015 to 2025, with 15 dates a year (17 November to 1 December), readings between 6:00 and 10:59 PM Phuket time. In a single year the number of rainy dates in that window ranged from 3 to 11 of 15.

### How we counted

We downloaded the observations for station VTSP from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, converted them to Asia/Bangkok time, kept readings stamped 18:00 to 22:59 for 17 November to 1 December of each year from 2015 to 2025, and counted a date as rainy when any reading in that window had a present-weather code containing RA or TS (rain, or thunderstorm including nearby thunderstorm). The airport sits in the north of the island, so a report of rain there does not mean rain at Cherngtalay. An observation count is not a rainfall depth, and 11 years is a sample, not a forecast. For the wider pattern, see our hour-by-hour count in [what to do in Phuket when it rains](/journal/phuket-rainy-season-evening).

The practical reading: carry a small umbrella, choose a krathong that can take a splash, and keep a table indoors for the second half of the night.

## What should a krathong be made of?

**A krathong made of banana trunk or banana leaf and flowers is the one to choose.** Phuket 101 advises banana tree slices or bread dough and says to avoid styrofoam. Kata Rocks describes banana leaf, flowers and other biodegradable materials as the practice encouraged across Thailand and polystyrene as harmful once in the water. Both are guides, not rules, and we found no 2026 Phuket-wide regulation. Ask the vendor what a krathong is made of before you buy.
