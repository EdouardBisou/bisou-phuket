## When is Bisou Phuket open at Christmas 2026?

**Bisou Phuket is open on Christmas Eve, Thursday 24 December 2026, and on Christmas Day, Friday 25 December, from 5:00 PM, with the kitchen open until midnight.** On a normal night we open at 6:00 PM and take the last food order around 11 PM, so for these two nights the doors open an hour earlier and the kitchen stays open an hour later.

That gives two kinds of Christmas Eve. Come at 5 PM with the children, when an early table suits them best. Or take the beach and the sunset first, change, and still sit down to a full French dinner at 10 PM.

## How late can I eat on Christmas Eve?

**At Bisou Phuket the kitchen takes orders until midnight on 24 and 25 December 2026.** On an ordinary night, most kitchens around Laguna and Bang Tao stop serving food by 11 PM, which is what we found when we checked them for our guide to [dinner outside your Laguna resort](/journal/dinner-off-resort-laguna-phuket). So on Christmas Eve, a 10 PM table is no rush here: the kitchen keeps taking orders for two more hours.

If your evening starts late anyway, our guide to [late night dining in Bang Tao](/journal/late-night-dining-bang-tao) covers the rest of the year.

## Can I bring children on Christmas Eve?

**Yes. Children are welcome at Bisou Phuket on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, with no minimum age and high chairs available.** The 5 PM opening is the one to book with children. On a normal night the room is at its calmest early and gets livelier as the evening goes on, with the music lifting through the night. Tell us the children's ages when you book so we can seat you in the right corner.

## What should I wear to Christmas dinner at Bisou?

**The dress code at Bisou Phuket is smart casual, with no flip-flops.** A shirt or a dress, closed shoes or smart sandals, and no jacket needed. The whole restaurant is indoors, so leave the beach at the beach.

## Who is Bisou Phuket?

**Bisou Phuket is a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar in Cherngtalay, opened in 2025 by chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne, with head chef Manon Bares running the kitchen and general manager Louis Chartier looking after the room.** Bisou means kiss in French and the tagline is simple & sexy. Inside there is a kiss wall, a spiral staircase, candlelight, and a bar you actually want to sit at.
