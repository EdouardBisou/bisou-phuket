The best restaurants in Phuket for dinner in November 2026 are [PRU](https://www.prurestaurant.com) at Trisara (two MICHELIN Stars), [Jaras](https://phuket.intercontinental.com/dining/jaras) in Kamala and [Omemo](https://www.omemo-phuket.com) in Kathu (one Star each), then [Wagyu Steakhouse](https://wagyuphuket.com) in Surin and [Bisou](/) in Cherngtalay on the west coast. The night matters as much as the name: of the twelve restaurants below with published opening days, only six serve dinner every night of the week, and PRU and Omemo are closed on Sunday and Monday.

We run Bisou, so we say where we fit and link to the others. Every opening day and hour below comes from the restaurant's own page or its MICHELIN Guide listing, read on 22 November 2026.

## The starred tables: PRU, Jaras and Omemo

Phuket has three MICHELIN-starred restaurants in the 2027 guide announced on 10 November 2026: PRU with two Stars, and Jaras and Omemo with one each. The [MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2027](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/michelin-guide-thailand-2027) covers a record 507 restaurants nationwide, and its [Phuket directory](https://guide.michelin.com/en/th/phuket-region/phuket/restaurants) lists 57.

**PRU** is Phuket's first two-star restaurant and the first outside Bangkok and its surrounding provinces. It sits at Trisara on Srisoonthorn Road in Cherngtalay and serves one tasting menu, no à la carte: the PRU Experience is THB 7,500++ for dinner, with a non-refundable deposit of THB 5,000 per person at booking, deducted from the final bill. Dinner is Tuesday to Saturday in four seatings between 5:45 and 7:00 PM. Chef and co-owner Jimmy Ophorst cooks from the restaurant's own farm, and the MICHELIN Guide names a dish pairing durian with caviar and sea grapes as its signature. Book through [prurestaurant.com](https://www.prurestaurant.com).

**Jaras** is a contemporary Southern Thai restaurant at the InterContinental Phuket Resort on Kamala Beach, promoted from MICHELIN Selected to one Star in the 2027 guide. It is open daily, with à la carte from noon to 10 PM (last order 9:30 PM) and a nine-course dinner from 6:00 to 10:00 PM at THB 3,850++ on its published menu (confirm the price when you book), reserved by 4:00 PM the day before. Chef Chalermwut "Nui" Srivorakul cooks a zero-waste kanom jeen and miang pla. In 2025 Time Out Phuket described it as ["one of Phuket's very best Southern Thai dining experiences"](https://www.timeout.com/phuket/restaurants/5-upscale-restaurants-in-phuket-you-dont-want-to-miss). See [phuket.intercontinental.com](https://phuket.intercontinental.com/dining/jaras).

**Omemo** is a Swiss contemporary restaurant on the second floor of its building in Kathu, new to the MICHELIN Guide in 2027 with one Star. It serves dinner Tuesday to Saturday from 6:00 to 11:00 PM, with the last tasting menu at 9:00 PM and the last à la carte order at 10:00 PM, and it is closed for dinner on Sunday and Monday. Reserve on [omemo-phuket.com](https://www.omemo-phuket.com).

## Tasting menus without a Star: The Smokaccia Laboratory, Royd and L'Arôme by the Sea

Three more restaurants serve a structured tasting menu and appear in the MICHELIN Guide 2027 directory without a Star: The Smokaccia Laboratory in Surin, Royd in Phuket Old Town and L'Arôme by the Sea in Kalim.

**The Smokaccia Laboratory** is an Italian-led "laboratory" built around sourdough, at 116/4 Moo 3 in Surin, run by chef Luca Mascolo and co-founder Brigitta Houkamp, with Massimo Zoz as sommelier. It serves nine courses at THB 5,500 or eighteen courses at THB 6,900, both before 7 percent tax and 10 percent service, with a deposit of THB 2,500 per person and no cancellations. Dinner is Thursday to Monday, with seatings between 6:00 and 7:30 PM, and it is closed on Tuesday and Wednesday. See [thesmokaccia.com](https://thesmokaccia.com).

**Royd** is a Southern Thai tasting restaurant at 95/1 Dibuk Road in Phuket Old Town. Chef Suwijak "Mond" Kunghae won the MICHELIN Young Chef Award in 2026. Dinner is Thursday to Monday in two seatings, at 5:00 and 8:15 PM: eight courses at THB 3,950 or five courses at THB 3,290, both with service and tax added. See [restaurantroyd.com](https://restaurantroyd.com).

**L'Arôme by the Sea** is a seafood-led contemporary French tasting menu at 247/5 Phrabaramee Road, on the cliff above Kalim Bay, with executive chef Maksym Chukanov. Its own site publishes no opening hours and other guides disagree, so confirm the night and the time when you book through [the restaurant](https://en.laromegroup.com/laromebythesea).

## West coast dinners: Cherngtalay, Surin and Bang Tao

The west coast has the most flexible dinners on the island: five restaurants within a short drive of each other, all of which open every night or close only on Monday. This is the stretch where a table of friends can book on the day.

**Bisou** is a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar at No.4 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road in Cherngtalay, a short drive from Laguna, Boat Avenue and Bang Tao Beach. It opened in 2025 and was founded by chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne, with head chef Manon Bares in the kitchen. Dinner is nightly from 6:00 PM, the last food order is around 11 PM and the cocktail bar runs to midnight, so a late table is possible on any night of the week. The cooking is French and plated to share, and the full evening is covered in [the Bisou guide](/journal/bisou-phuket-guide).

**Wagyu Steakhouse** is a two-floor steakhouse at 106/46 Moo 3 in Surin, with Josper and beech-oven grills and dry-aged beef. It is open nightly from 5:30 PM to midnight, and steaks on the official menu run from THB 590 per 100 g for a Tallawanta Angus rib eye to THB 1,650 per 100 g for Miyazaki A4 tenderloin, plus 10 percent service and 7 percent VAT. It is listed in the MICHELIN Guide. See [wagyuphuket.com](https://wagyuphuket.com), and our [steakhouse guide](/journal/best-steakhouses-phuket) for the whole category.

**Ember Steakhouse** cooks over open fire at 323/16 Moo 2 on Bang Tao Beach, open daily from noon to 11 PM with the last kitchen order at 10:30 PM. Chef Gautam Sethi's signatures include New Zealand lamb chops and an Ebony Black Angus tomahawk. See [embersteakhousephuket.com](https://embersteakhousephuket.com).

**Benny's Cocktails & Grill** is a charcoal grill and classic cocktail bar at 5/6 Soi Had Bangtao, between Surin and Bang Tao, open Tuesday to Sunday from 5:00 PM to midnight and closed on Monday. Founder Benedikt "Benny" De Bellis publishes a weekly programme, including Steak & Jazz on Wednesdays from 7:00 PM. See [bennysphuket.com](https://bennysphuket.com).

**Little Paris** is a French bistro at 49/47 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road on Boat Avenue, open every day from 8:30 AM to midnight, which makes it the easiest all-day option in this guide. See [littleparisphuket.com](https://www.littleparisphuket.com). For the French category as a whole, read [the best French restaurants in Phuket](/journal/best-french-restaurants-phuket).

## Southern Thai and Old Town: lob-baan and Blue Elephant

For Phuket's own cooking, book lob-baan for Southern Thai on the east side and Blue Elephant for royal Thai in Phuket Old Town. They sit far from the beaches, so plan the drive.

**lob-baan** is a Southern Thai restaurant at 189/3 Ko Kaeo, east of Phuket Town, new to the Bib Gourmand list in the 2027 guide. Chef Natdanai Kitkorsup also won the 2027 MICHELIN Young Chef Award. Dinner is 5:00 to 10:00 PM Monday and Thursday to Sunday, closed Tuesday and Wednesday, and the MICHELIN Guide lists booking by phone on +66 82 918 9874. See its [MICHELIN listing](https://guide.michelin.com/en/phuket-region/phuket/restaurant/lob-baan).

**Blue Elephant** serves royal and Peranakan Thai cooking in a restored Sino-Portuguese governor's mansion at 96 Krabi Road in Phuket Old Town. Its own site gives daily hours of 9:00 AM to 10:30 PM. Master Chef Nooror's signature pairs Thai tamarind with French Landes foie gras. See [blueelephant.com/phuket](https://www.blueelephant.com/phuket).

## How to choose your dinner in Phuket

For a starred dinner from Tuesday to Saturday choose PRU, Jaras or Omemo, and for a late table on any night choose Bisou or Wagyu Steakhouse. The rest depends on the night and the mood.

- **A starred dinner on a Tuesday to Saturday:** PRU, Jaras or Omemo.
- **A starred dinner on a Monday:** Jaras, the only one open.
- **A Sunday or Monday tasting menu in Surin or Old Town:** The Smokaccia Laboratory or Royd, both open on those nights and closed Tuesday and Wednesday.
- **A late table, any night of the week:** Bisou, with the last food order around 11 PM, or Wagyu Steakhouse, open to midnight.
- **A steak:** Wagyu Steakhouse, Ember Steakhouse or Benny's. Our full comparison is in the [steakhouse guide](/journal/best-steakhouses-phuket).
- **Southern Thai cooking:** lob-baan, Royd or Jaras.
- **A table that books on the day:** the west coast restaurants that open every night, and Bisou accepts walk-ins when there is room, though booking is better. More options near Laguna and Bang Tao are in [where to eat in Bang Tao and Cherngtalay](/journal/where-to-eat-bang-tao) and [where to eat late in Bang Tao](/journal/late-night-dining-bang-tao).
- **A celebration:** [Christmas Eve and Christmas Day](/journal/christmas-dinner-phuket), [New Year's Eve](/journal/new-years-eve-dinner-phuket) and [a birthday dinner](/journal/birthday-dinner-phuket) each have their own guide.

In high season, book the starred restaurants and the tasting menus well ahead. The booking calendar for December and January is in [how to book restaurants in Phuket in high season](/journal/book-restaurants-phuket-high-season).

Bisou serves modern French dishes to share from 6:00 PM every night, in a room with a kiss wall, a spiral staircase and a cocktail bar. The kitchen takes its last food order around 11 PM and the bar pours until midnight. Prices below are from the live [menu](/menu) as of November 2026, before service charge and VAT.

- **Black truffle pasta, fagottini, Paris ham and parmesan:** THB 790.

- **Beef Wellington with foie gras and mushroom:** THB 2,790, carved at the table.
- **Prunier caviar service, 30 g:** THB 2,990.

The dress code is smart casual, with no flip-flops. Children are welcome and high chairs are available, and an early seating suits a family best because the room turns adult as the music lifts. ## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best restaurants in Phuket for dinner?

As of November 2026, the best restaurants in Phuket for dinner include PRU at Trisara (two MICHELIN Stars), Jaras in Kamala and Omemo in Kathu (one Star each), Wagyu Steakhouse in Surin, and Bisou, a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar in Cherngtalay. For Southern Thai cooking, lob-baan in Ko Kaeo holds a Bib Gourmand.

### Which restaurants in Phuket are open for dinner on a Monday?

On a Monday in Phuket, Jaras, Wagyu Steakhouse, Bisou, Ember Steakhouse, Little Paris, Blue Elephant, The Smokaccia Laboratory, Royd and lob-baan serve dinner as of 22 November 2026. PRU, Omemo and Benny's Cocktails & Grill are closed on Monday.

### Is there a MICHELIN-starred restaurant in Phuket?

Yes. The MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2027, announced on 10 November 2026, gives Phuket three starred restaurants: PRU at Trisara with two Stars, and Jaras in Kamala and Omemo in Kathu with one Star each. PRU is the first two-star restaurant in Phuket.

### What time does the kitchen close at restaurants in Phuket?

It varies by restaurant. As of November 2026, Bisou in Cherngtalay takes its last food order around 11 PM, Ember Steakhouse on Bang Tao Beach at 10:30 PM, Jaras in Kamala at 9:30 PM for à la carte, and Omemo in Kathu serves its last tasting menu at 9:00 PM. Wagyu Steakhouse in Surin is open until midnight.

### Do I need to book dinner in Phuket?

Book the starred and tasting-menu restaurants ahead: PRU takes a non-refundable THB 5,000 per person deposit, The Smokaccia Laboratory a THB 2,500 per person deposit with no cancellations, and Jaras asks for its nine-course dinner by 4:00 PM the day before. Bisou in Cherngtalay accepts walk-ins when there is room, and booking is better.

### What is Bisou Phuket?

Bisou is a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar at No.4 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, Choeng Thale, Thalang, Phuket 83110, founded in 2025 by chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne. Dinner is nightly from 6:00 PM, with the last food order around 11 PM and the bar open to midnight. Reservations are at book.bistrochat.com/bisou-phuket.
