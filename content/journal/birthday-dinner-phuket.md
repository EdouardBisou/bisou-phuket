Bisou, a modern French restaurant in Cherngtalay, takes 10 percent off the whole bill for the whole table when one guest shows an ID proving they were born in the current month. It applies on any night of that month, not only on the day itself, and it covers everyone sitting at the table. Searching Phuket, Bang Tao, Laguna and Cherngtalay in August 2026, we could not find another restaurant on the island publishing a birthday-month discount of any kind. This guide sets out that offer in full, then everything else Phuket actually publishes for a birthday.

Most Phuket birthday pages sell a feeling and hide the terms. The three shapes that genuinely exist here are nightclub bottle packages on Bangla Road, private buyouts with a minimum spend, and restaurants that will put a candle in something if you mention it. Knowing which one you are booking is most of the work.

## What is the birthday offer at Bisou Phuket?

Ten percent off the entire bill, for the entire table, during the whole of your birthday month, on presentation of an ID.

The terms are deliberately short, because a birthday offer with a page of conditions is not an offer.

- **Who it covers**: the whole table, not just the birthday guest.
- **What it covers**: the whole bill.
- **When**: any night during the calendar month of the birthday. A birthday on the 3rd is still valid on the 27th.
- **What you show**: an ID, passport or Thai ID card, with the date of birth on it. Show it when the bill comes, or mention it when you book.

That is unusually broad for the island. The comparable offers we could verify are structured the other way round: you pay a package price and receive things back. TaiPan on Bangla Road publishes birthday packages at 10,000, 15,000 and 20,000 THB for bronze, silver and gold, which include a VIP sofa, discounted bottle service, snacks, a cake and a T-shirt, with one complimentary sparkling wine for six guests and two for ten, available between 21:30 and 1:00, and no ID required. That is a nightclub table, not a dinner, and it is the right thing for a certain kind of birthday.

One correction worth publishing, because the claim is repeated widely: **Dinner in the Sky Phuket is often reported to give a 10 percent birthday discount. No such discount appears on its own pricing page.** What that page does publish is 4,900 THB per person for the sunset seating from 17:30 to 18:30, 3,900 THB for dinner from 19:30 to 20:30, and 1,900 THB for cocktails only, with birthday extras limited to customised messages, cake options and group packages. Do not turn up expecting a discount that the operator does not advertise.

## Can you bring your own cake to a restaurant in Phuket?

At Bisou, yes, and it costs nothing. No Phuket restaurant we could find publishes a cakeage fee at all, in either direction, so the honest answer for everywhere else is that you have to ask.

Cakeage exists internationally to recover the dessert the kitchen did not sell and the service time the cake takes. There is no documented Phuket norm, no published range, and no venue on the island stating a figure on its own site as of August 2026. Anyone quoting you a standard Phuket cakeage price is guessing.

At Bisou there is no fee, and the team will cut the cake and plate it for your guests rather than handing you a knife. If you are bringing one, tell the restaurant when you book so there is somewhere cold to keep it.

Where to buy one, since most visitors ask: ready-made cakes are stocked at Big C, Villa Market, Tops and the larger Lotus's stores. Custom bakeries on the island include Ilona Cake Phuket and Cakes Shop Sweet Box. Ordinary birthday candles are sold at 7-Eleven and Lotus's, and number-shaped candles turn up in mall stationery shops such as Central.

## The birthday dinner at Bisou, in practice

A modern French restaurant and cocktail bar on Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, a short drive from Laguna, Boat Avenue and Bang Tao Beach, with parking at the rear via the side street.

Bisou opened in 2025, founded by chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne, with Manon Bares running the kitchen and Louis Chartier looking after the room. The food is plated to share, which suits a birthday table better than a plate each: truffle French toast to start, a house beef Wellington, a whole seabass in salt crust flambéed at the table. Dinner runs nightly from 6:00 PM to 1:00 AM and the cocktail bar runs throughout, so a table that starts at eight can still be a bar at midnight without moving.

The birthday plate arrives without being asked for: Happy Birthday piped freehand in dark chocolate across a large plate, quenelles, edible flowers, a candle. It is not something you unlock by ordering a set menu, and it is not on the bill.

## When to book, and what to wear

Book earlier than you think between November and April, and dress smart casual anywhere decent.

Phuket's high season runs from November to April, with the peak from mid-December through February, when the island sits at 27 to 28 degrees and hotel rates run 30 to 50 percent above low season. The two busiest weeks of the year are New Year and Chinese New Year, when a west coast table on a Saturday is genuinely hard to get at short notice. Outside those weeks, a few days is usually enough for a table of two to six, and a week or more for anything larger.

The dress code norm at Phuket's better restaurants is smart casual: no beach shorts, no flip-flops. That is worth saying out loud to a group arriving from Bang Tao Beach at seven.

Bang Tao itself is a 6 km beach on the west coast, roughly halfway between the airport and Patong, with the Laguna complex behind it and Boat Avenue, and its Friday market, just inland. Cherngtalay sits between them, which is why a birthday dinner here works for a group scattered across three different resorts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which restaurant in Phuket gives a birthday discount?

Bisou in Cherngtalay takes 10 percent off the whole bill for the whole table during your birthday month, on presentation of an ID showing the date of birth. As of August 2026, no other Phuket restaurant publishes a birthday-month discount. Other published birthday offers on the island are nightclub packages, such as TaiPan on Bangla Road at 10,000 to 20,000 THB, or private buyouts with minimum spends.

### Do I need to celebrate on the exact day to get the birthday discount at Bisou?

No. The offer applies on any night during the calendar month of your birthday. A birthday on the 3rd is still valid on the 27th of the same month. Bring an ID or passport showing the date of birth.

### Can I bring my own birthday cake to a restaurant in Phuket?

At Bisou there is no cakeage fee, and the team will cut the cake and serve it to your guests. No Phuket restaurant we could find publishes a cakeage fee on its own website, so anywhere else you should ask before the night. Tell the restaurant when you book so the cake can be kept cold.

### How much is a birthday dinner in Phuket?

It depends entirely on the format. A nightclub birthday package at TaiPan runs 10,000 to 20,000 THB for the table. Dinner in the Sky is 3,900 to 4,900 THB per person. A private buyout at Nitan needs 60,000 THB++ minimum spend and a 40,000 THB deposit. A normal restaurant dinner in Cherngtalay costs far less, and at Bisou 10 percent comes off the whole bill during your birthday month.

### How far ahead should I book a birthday dinner in Phuket?

A few days is usually enough for a small table outside high season. From November to April, and especially between mid-December and February, book at least a week ahead, and considerably earlier for New Year and Chinese New Year, the two busiest weeks on the island.
