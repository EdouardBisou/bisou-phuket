**Bisou Phuket in Cherngtalay has three black truffle dishes on its menu: black truffle pasta (fagottini, Paris ham, parmesan) at THB 790, black truffle French toast at THB 390, and a pan-scallop, foie gras and truffle bite at THB 490.** Bisou Phuket serves its food to share, and these three dishes are on the current menu.

Black truffle is a winter ingredient in France. Here is what each dish is, how the season runs, and how to book.

## What black truffle dishes does Bisou Phuket serve?

**Three plates on the current menu carry black truffle, from a single-bite starter to a full pasta.** Prices are from the menu as of 16 November 2026 and can change.

| Dish | What it is | Price |
|---|---|---|
| French toast black truffle | French toast with black truffle | THB 390 |
| Pan-scallop, foie gras, truffle bite | Pan-scallop, foie gras and truffle bite | THB 490 |
| Black truffle pasta | Fagottini, Paris ham, parmesan | THB 790 |

The French toast is one of Bisou's signatures, and it also comes with caviar (THB 990) or with foie gras and smoked eel (THB 490). The pasta is listed as black truffle pasta: fagottini, Paris ham, parmesan. The cocktail bar runs to midnight.

## When is black truffle season?

**The classic black truffle of France, the Périgord truffle, is dug in winter, roughly from mid-November to mid-March in the Dordogne.** [Lascaux Dordogne](https://www.lascaux-dordogne.com/en/sejourner/se-regaler/les-produits/les-truffes/) gives mid-November to mid-March for the truffle fields from Sarlat to Sorges, while [Sarlat Tourisme](https://en.sarlat-tourisme.com/the-gourmet-perigord-page/the-black-perigord-truffle/) gives December to February. Sources differ on the exact dates, which is why we say "roughly".

The menu lists black truffle without stating an origin.

## How do I order the truffle dishes?

**Ask the team at the table, and order the truffle dishes alongside the rest of the menu.** The same menu has the beef Wellington, carved at the table (THB 2,990), and the whole seabass in salt crust, flambéed tableside (THB 1,290).

Warm sourdough and French butter come to every table at no charge, as we explain in [bread and butter in Phuket](/journal/bread-and-butter-phuket). Tell the team about allergies when you book, and vegetarian and vegan guests are welcome.

## Where is Bisou Phuket and how do I book?

**Bisou Phuket is at No.4 Bandon-Cherngtalay Rd, Choeng Thale, Thalang, Phuket 83110, a short drive from Laguna, Boat Avenue and Bang Tao Beach.** It is open nightly from 6:00 PM, with the last food order around 11 PM and the bar open to midnight. The dress code is smart casual, no flip-flops, and valet parking is complimentary.

Tables are booked on BistroChat at [book.bistrochat.com/bisou-phuket](https://book.bistrochat.com/bisou-phuket); walk-ins are welcome when there is room, and a booking is better. The whole restaurant is indoors.

Bisou Phuket is a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar, founded in 2025 by chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne, with Manon Bares as head chef.
