## When is high season in Phuket, and which weeks are the peak?

**High season in Phuket runs from November to April by Phuket 101's calendar, with the peak from mid-December to February.** Phuket 101, a long-running local guide updated in July 2026, splits it into a November to early December transition, a mid-December to February peak, and a hotter March to May tail that includes Songkran on 13 to 15 April. [Phuket Expat Guide](https://phuketexpatguide.com/blog/phuket-fine-dining-restaurants/), which writes about dining, draws the line earlier and says November to March. The sources differ by a month at the edge.

The scale is measurable. Phuket International Airport handled 17,474,064 passengers in 2025, up 1.5% on 2024 and about 96% of the pre-pandemic level of 2019 ([The Phuket Express](https://thephuketexpress.com/2026/01/14/phuket-airport-reports-strong-recovery-in-passenger-and-flight-numbers/), January 2026). Within the season, Phuket 101 says the most popular beachfront hotels sell out weeks ahead, especially over New Year and Chinese New Year, and that prices can be even higher in those peak weeks ([Phuket 101](https://phuket101.net/high-season-in-phuket)). Phuket Expat Guide, writing about restaurants, names Christmas/New Year and Chinese New Year as its peak weeks.

## How far ahead should you book a restaurant in Phuket in high season?

**Book ahead for all of high season, and for the three peak stretches book at least two to four weeks out, which is the most specific range we found.** Phuket Expat Guide (published April 2026) advises always booking in advance for high season, November to March. For Christmas, New Year and Chinese New Year the same guide says many top restaurants fill up two to four weeks ahead, so book at least that far out. It also gives shorter figures for two named venues, Kata Rocks and Catch Beach Club, in its FAQ.

Hotels run on a longer clock. Phuket 101 advises booking at least one to two months ahead for December and January, especially for beachfront hotels, and says two to three weeks ahead is usually enough for November, March or April. It adds that over New Year and Chinese New Year popular hotels sell out even earlier. Restaurant guidance is shorter than hotel guidance, but both say to book earlier for New Year and Chinese New Year.

Our reading, and it is advice and not a Bisou policy: if your trip includes Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve or Chinese New Year, aim to book that dinner by the four-week date in the table below, the far end of the published range, and earlier if you want a resort's early-bird price. For every other evening of the season, the guide's advice is simply to book ahead, so do not leave a celebration to the day.

## Which way of booking works best in Phuket?

**Use the restaurant's own website or booking page first, then a message app or a call.** Phuket Expat Guide notes that most of the top restaurants it lists now accept reservations through their website, WhatsApp or Google, and says one of them, Bampot Kitchen & Bar in Phuket Town, still works by phone or walk-in outside peak season. The practical difference in high season is the written confirmation: a booking you can see on your phone is easier to change than a spoken one.

Whichever channel you use, say who is coming (children, a birthday, an allergy) and what time you want, because those three change the table you are given. Ask about deposits and cancellation at the same moment, because terms vary; the two resort examples above publish theirs.

**Book early or late if you can; the middle of the evening is the slot everyone asks for.** That is practical advice and not a published statistic. Two things give you room at Bisou Phuket:
