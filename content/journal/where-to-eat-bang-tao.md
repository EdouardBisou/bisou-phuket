Bang Tao has quietly become one of the best corners of Phuket to eat in, and the reason is density: two open-air centres at the Laguna gate, a strip of neighbourhood kitchens along Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, and a handful of genuinely serious independents a few kilometres inland, all within about ten minutes of each other. Below are 14 places worth your evening, with addresses and hours, grouped by the kind of night you want rather than by cuisine.

Everything here was checked in August 2026. Where a restaurant's own site and the listings disagree on closing time, we say so rather than pick the convenient number.

## Where is Bang Tao, and what is Cherngtalay?

Cherngtalay, written Choeng Thale on maps, is a subdistrict of Thalang in the middle-west of Phuket, just south of the airport. It takes in Bang Tao Beach, the island's longest, with Surin and Layan further up the coast. Most people call the whole neighbourhood Bang Tao, or Laguna after the resort complex behind the sand. It is greener and calmer than the south of the island, and increasingly somewhere people come specifically to eat.

## Boat Avenue and Porto de Phuket: the walkable centre

Two open-air centres face each other at the Laguna entrance on Bandon-Cherngtalay Road. This is where most visitors eat, and it is the right answer when nobody can agree.

### Bocconcino

**21/14 Lagoon Road, on the Laguna side of Boat Avenue.** A wooden terrace out over the lake, bread, focaccia and pastries baked on site every morning, handmade pasta and a wood-fired oven. Chef Massimo Pettinau, a Sardinian. Open daily from noon to 10pm. The best terrace in the area for a long lunch.

### Joya Restaurant & Cocktail Bar

**49/21 Boat Avenue.** Contemporary European under executive chef Andrey Chetvertnov, a Le Cordon Bleu Paris graduate. It opens at 8am and runs to midnight, so it covers coffee, a long lunch and a nightcap at one address. Books through TableCheck. The dressier option on the strip.

### The Farmer and The Fisherman

**Porto de Phuket.** The Phuket outpost of the Ginger Farm Kitchen group from Chiang Mai, built on farm produce and the day's catch. Order the tom yum paella, a whole pan of seafood rice built on tom yum, around 995 baht. Open daily 11am to 11pm, and it takes bookings through Hungry Hub.

### Tu Kab Khao

**Porto de Phuket.** Phuket Peranakan home cooking: crab curry eaten with thin rice noodles, moo hong pork belly stewed dark and sweet, southern chilli pastes. Open daily from 11am, though sources disagree on the evening close, so call ahead if you are eating late. One thing worth knowing: the MICHELIN Bib Gourmand belongs to the original Tu Kab Khao in Phuket Town, not to this branch.

### El Gaucho

**Porto de Phuket.** An Argentinian steakhouse, flame-grilled Angus and a long international list. There is a daily lunch deal until 3pm that undercuts the dinner bill considerably. Open 11:30am to 11pm, last food order 10pm. The priciest table in the centre.

### Holey Artisan Bakery Cafe

**Mercado Hall, Porto de Phuket, unit FB109.** Long-fermented sourdough and pastries out of the oven from 7am. The one place near Laguna for a proper loaf rather than a hotel croissant. Open daily 7am to 9pm.

### Fun Friday Avenue night market

**Along the Boat Avenue strip, Fridays only, from 4pm until around 9 or 10pm.** One evening a week the pedestrian stretch fills with stalls: whole fish barbecued in a salt crust, grilled meats, fried noodles, som tum, smoothies, a cocktail bar. The best cheap eating in the area, and worth planning your week around.

## The Cherngtalay strip: neighbourhood kitchens on the main road

Along Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, away from the centres, is where people who live here eat. Lower prices, longer histories, no view.

### Black Cat Bar & Restaurant

**104 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road.** A French-Thai bistro open since 1996 under Philippe, a French chef, and his Thai wife Meow, which is why a French onion soup, a pork chop with mushroom sauce and a house crème brûlée sit on the same menu as the Thai dishes. Three decades on this road is the credential.

### Siam Sunset Restaurant

**100 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, directly opposite Porto de Phuket and next door to Black Cat.** Proper curries, stir-fries and Thai soups, whole fried fish, and a crispy melinjo leaf salad that is a real southern Thai plate rather than a tourist one. It serves straight through the day. Some vegetarian options, though not a separate menu.

### Chernglay Kitchen

**50/77 Moo 4 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road**, about 2 km north of the Black Cat stretch, set back under cover. The neighbourhood workhorse: very large portions, very low prices, fresh seafood cooked to order, and a clams soup regulars come back for. Menu in Thai and English. There is a karaoke setup rather than a live band, so pick your night.

## Inland, on the Srisoonthorn corridor: the serious independents

These are a five to twelve minute drive inland and are not in Cherngtalay, which most guides fail to mention. They are worth the taxi.

### Suay Cherngtalay

**177/99 Moo 6, tambon Si Sunthon**, about 5 km inland from Bang Tao Beach. Modern Thai from chef-owner Tammasak "Noi" Chootong, who has cooked under the Suay name since opening the first one in Phuket Town in 2010, running Thai flavours through European technique. Not to be confused with the Suay at Porto de Phuket.

### Nitan

**177/38 Srisoonthorn Road, tambon Si Sunthon**, roughly 300 m from Suay and about 3 km from the Cherngtalay strip. A Thai-European tasting menu in a genuinely serious independent dining room rather than a hotel restaurant, built partly on produce from the restaurant's own farm in Surat Thani.

### Kin-Kub-Ei

**81 Srisoonthorn Road, tambon Si Sunthon**, a 10 to 12 minute drive inland, about 6 km from Bang Tao Beach. A southern Thai family kitchen where Khun Tipsuda "Tubtim" Khanchaijatuwit cooks her own family's recipes to order in a shaded garden room. Order the pla tod krueng, deep-fried fish with curry paste. The most local table on this list.

## The beachfront: sunset rather than dinner

Along the sand you will find the beach clubs: loungers, long cocktail lists, the sun going down over the Andaman. Read them as the daytime-into-golden-hour end of Bang Tao rather than as dinner destinations, since the food is the supporting act and the view you paid for disappears at dusk.

## Bisou: the modern French table in the middle of it

Then there is the dinner you plan around. A room you book, dress for a little, and settle into.

[Bisou](/) is a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar at No.4 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, a short drive from Laguna, Boat Avenue and Bang Tao Beach, with parking at the rear via the side street. It opened in 2025, founded by chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne, with Manon Bares running the kitchen and Louis Chartier looking after the room.

The [menu](/menu) is plated to share, so a table orders together rather than each to their own: truffle French toast to begin, a house beef Wellington carved at the table, a whole seabass baked in salt crust and flambéed beside you. Dinner runs nightly from 6:00 PM to 1:00 AM with the cocktail bar throughout, which matters here: most kitchens on this list stop serving food between 10 and 11 PM.

Bread and French butter come to every table before anything else, at no charge, and the [birthday plate](/journal/birthday-dinner-phuket) arrives without being asked for if someone is celebrating.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where should I eat in Bang Tao, Phuket?

Start with Boat Avenue and Porto de Phuket at the Laguna entrance, where Bocconcino, Joya, The Farmer and The Fisherman, Tu Kab Khao and El Gaucho sit within a short walk. For neighbourhood Thai, Black Cat and Siam Sunset are on Bandon-Cherngtalay Road. For a French dinner you plan around, Bisou is on the same road, open nightly from 6 PM.

### Where do locals eat in Cherngtalay?

Along Bandon-Cherngtalay Road rather than in the centres. Chernglay Kitchen does very large portions of fresh seafood at low prices, Siam Sunset serves proper southern Thai through the day, and Black Cat has been run by the same French-Thai couple since 1996. Kin-Kub-Ei, ten minutes inland on Srisoonthorn Road, is the most local kitchen of the three.

### What night is the Boat Avenue market in Bang Tao?

Friday. Fun Friday Avenue runs along the Boat Avenue strip from about 4pm until 9 or 10pm, with stalls doing salt-crusted barbecued fish, grilled meats, noodles, som tum and cocktails. It is the best cheap eating in the area.

### Where can you eat late in Bang Tao?

Most kitchens here stop serving food between 10 and 11 PM. Joya runs to midnight, and Bisou on Bandon-Cherngtalay Road serves dinner to 1 AM with the cocktail bar open throughout. Beyond that the options are the 24-hour convenience stores on the main road.

### How far is Boat Avenue from Bang Tao Beach?

About five minutes by car. Boat Avenue and Porto de Phuket sit at the entrance to the Laguna complex on Bandon-Cherngtalay Road, with the beach directly behind the resorts. The inland restaurants on Srisoonthorn Road are a further five to ten minutes.
