Eating late in Bang Tao and Cherngtalay takes a little planning, because kitchens close at different times and a last food order is not the same as a closing time. [Bisou](/), a modern French restaurant and cocktail bar at No.4 Bandon-Cherngtalay Road in Choeng Thale, takes its last food order around 11 PM and keeps the cocktail bar open to midnight. This guide sets that next to named kitchens nearby and the closing times they publish, as of October 2026.

The point of the page is practical: know which kitchens are still cooking after 10 PM before you drive there.

## The short answer: where to get a late dinner

Bisou takes its last food order around 11 PM, and the cocktail bar stays open to midnight. The restaurant is open nightly from 6 PM. Other late options in the area include JOYA and Issara on Boat Avenue, Mrs B Bar & Table and La Marée on Lagoon Road, and Eden by the Lake, all listing midnight closings in our [Bang Tao restaurant guide](/journal/where-to-eat-bang-tao).

Bisou sits a short drive inland from Bang Tao Beach, Laguna and Boat Avenue. The room is modern French, plated to share, founded in 2025 by chef Antoine Darquin and sommelier Théo Lavergne, with head chef Manon Bares in the kitchen and Louis Chartier looking after the room.

The signatures suit a later table because they are built to share: truffle French toast to start, a house beef Wellington carved at the table, a whole seabass baked in salt crust and flambéed in the room, and a black truffle pasta. See the [menu](/menu) for the current lineup and prices.

## What to check before a late dinner

A closing time and a last food order are different things. A restaurant that closes at midnight may stop taking food orders well before then, so ask for the last food order time when you book. At Bisou it is around 11 PM.

If you are coming off a beach-club afternoon or a late arrival, booking a seating you know the kitchen will honour is the difference between a real dinner and a missed one.
